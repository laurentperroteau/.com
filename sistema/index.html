Sistema

Sistema is a personal productivity tool that helps me plan and review my work from my Google Tasks and Google Calendar. It runs locally on my own computer and has a single user: me. It is not offered as a public service.

Privacy policy

Google data accessed

  • Google Tasks (read and write): to display my task lists and update tasks (create, complete, edit).
  • Google Calendar (read-only): to display my calendar events alongside my tasks.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide Sistema's features to its single user.
  • Data stays on my own computer. It is not sent to any server operated by Sistema.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising or for training AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
